# MI PASS Product Requirements

## Identity
MI PASS — My Society | My Services | My Life

## Product positioning
HL City's digital ecosystem, not a generic directory.

## Core actors
Resident, Provider, Business Owner, Service Manager, Admin, Super Admin.

## Critical workflow
Resident selects service -> submits requirement -> admin assigns provider -> authorized provider accepts -> service -> resident confirms -> verified review.

## Critical business rule
Only AUTHORIZED providers can receive service requests. This must be enforced on the backend.
